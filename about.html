ABOUT US

Who are we?

NorthRoy is a mining and natural resources company focused on the responsible exploration, development, and extraction of mineral resources. We are committed to creating long-term value through safe, efficient, and environmentally responsible mining practices.

  • Mineral exploration and resource evaluation
  • Geological and technical services
  • Supply and logistics management

ABOUT US

Our History

73 years ago, NorthRoy opened the door for people to become part of something extraordinary, an incredible natural resource discovered by five everyday men with an uncommon pioneering spirit. What began as a modest venture has since evolved into a global journey that has touched millions of lives.

Today, that legacy continues through products that contribute to the development of our world and investments that strengthen the communities around them. It creates employment that supports families and generates returns that help people plan for a more secure future.

Like many pioneers of their era, the founders ventured into the emerging world of mineral exploration in the Australian outback. They weren't trained geologists or experienced miners. They came from ordinary backgrounds as pastoralists, farm workers, and bookkeepers.

What they lacked in technical expertise, they made up for with determination, curiosity, and a willingness to take risks.

Above all, they were problem solvers, people who saw challenges as opportunities to discover what was possible.
