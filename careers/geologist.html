Basics & Benefits

  • Perth, WA
  • Full Time
  • Geology & Exploration
  • AUD 60.00 - 90.00 per hour
  • Accomodation, Flights & Meals Provided
  • Paid Annual Leaves & Bonuses
  • Visa Sponsorship Available

NorthRoy Resources is currently seeking Geologists for ongoing opportunities with one of our key clients to work across a range of established mining operations throughout Western Australia.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide geological support to the mining operation, including grade control, ore spotting and production activities
  • Undertake geological mapping, sampling and interpretation to improve understanding of the orebody
  • Support reconciliation, resource definition and short-term mine planning activities
  • Collaborate with Mining and Geology teams to deliver safe, efficient and commercially focused outcomes

Requirements

  • Tertiary qualification in Geology or a related discipline
  • Strong communication, analytical and problem-solving skills
  • White Card, with Working at Heights, Confined Space and Gas Testing tickets(or willing to obtain)
  • First Aid and CPR certification
  • Valid C-Class Driver’s Licence
  • Must have the necessary Valid Tickets(Qualifications) or Willing to obtain.
  • Valid Working Rights in Australia preferred.
